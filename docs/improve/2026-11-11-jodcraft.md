# JOÐcraft: evolve pass, 11 November 2026

Branch `claude/gifted-fermat-t7deyq`, restarted from `origin/main` as of #137. The owner's ask: on a phone, Taflan can be dragged sideways while scrolling down, which makes it annoying and unsmooth; fix it, and find and make whatever else improves the phone's evening. Nothing deployed.

## How it was done

A local Redis and two crew sign-ins made it possible, for the first time in these passes, to walk the site signed in with real signs on the board: long words, a long link, a wish, a question, answers, lanterns and items, posted through the site's own API from an emulated iPhone. Every page was then measured on three phone profiles, signed in and out: the page's scroll width, every element reaching past the screen's edge and what holds it, every text field's size, every control's tap size.

## The sideways scroll: two causes

1. **The drifting clouds.** On Taflan and Eftirlýst the band of night clouds slides 120 px east as the page scrolls. It was held by `overflow: clip` on the sky, which Chromium honours and which Safari evidently counts into the page's width anyway: the further down, the more room to the side. The sky's boxes and the flight's now use `overflow: hidden`, and the root has `overflow-x: hidden`.
2. **The zoom on a tap.** The answer field on every sign was 14 px. Safari zooms the page when a field under 16 px takes the keyboard and stays zoomed, after which every page pans sideways. The answer, the place picker and the panel's fields are 16 px where the finger is the pointer.

Neither can be reproduced in this sandbox, which has Chromium only: in it nothing overflowed before or after. The first is argued from what was measured (the clouds are the only thing wider than the screen on those pages, and only once scrolled) and the second from Safari's documented behaviour. **Both need a look on a real iPhone.**

## Also fixed

- **A strip dragged to its end stops there** rather than handing the drag to the page as a swipe back; the rooms, album, duel and dialogs scroll up and down only.
- **The hotbar's slot of the page already open scrolls it to the top.** It did nothing before.
- **Deila and the pin's three kinds are a thumb tall on touch**; a reply's Eyða sits level with its time.

## New

- **An address written in a note is a link.** On a sign or in an answer; shown without its scheme and cut short; the site's own pages open in place, others in a new tab. Only http and https, never one carrying a password. Try it: pin a note with a link in it. (`src/lib/note-links.ts`; six tests)
