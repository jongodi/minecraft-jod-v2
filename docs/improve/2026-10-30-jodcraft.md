# JOÐcraft: improve pass, 30 October 2026

Branch `improve/oct-30-map-immersion`, on `origin/main` as of #132. Focus: the 3D map, then anything broken, rough or flat elsewhere. Nothing pushed or deployed.

## Brief

- **What.** The landing page of a private Paper Minecraft server for eight friends, in Icelandic. Its one job: show the server is alive and let the crew walk into their world, from a phone or a desk.
- **Identity.** One evening in a badlands sunset, scrolled into night. Terracotta strata, oak planks, paper slips, iron chains, lantern glass; three pixel faces; amber only on what can be pressed; nothing soft-edged; motion in the game's whole steps.
- **The map.** BlueMap in JOÐ's clothes: a still of the town, the flight down with the scroll, the live viewer landing on the very view the flight ends on, the places as lanterns, the crew as heads, the rooms rising over it.
- **Stack.** Next 16, React 19, plain CSS on OKLCH tokens, vitest, Vercel. The viewer's skin is `public/bluemap-jod`; the viewer itself is BlueMap's own app, never edited.
- **Protected.** The sunset hero, the flight and its hand-over, the rail of places, the rooms over the world, the hotbar, the campfire, every URL and anchor. All verified intact.

## Fixed

- **A phone that left the whole screen before the map had drawn got a black frame.** The still faded out the moment the viewer said it was ready (`is-ready`), while a viewer paused before it ever drew holds a black canvas; the same happened when a browser refused the whole screen. The frame now knows whether the live map has been on screen since it was asked for (`is-unseen`): until it has, the still stands with its dusk and its lights and the viewer waits out of sight, and the lantern opens the whole screen again. A still on a phone also hides BlueMap's own keys, which pressed nothing. (`World.tsx`, `badlands.css`)
- **The board's stylesheet was preloaded on every page and never used there.** The 404 and the error screen imported it for the page frame, the pages' night sky and the death screen, and a sheet the root's not-found boundary imports is preloaded everywhere: the one console warning in the baseline, on the home page, the roll call, a night's page and the admin. Those rules moved into the sheet every one of those screens already loads. (`board.css`, `badlands.css`, `OffTrail.tsx`)
- **A missed players poll took every head off the land for five seconds.** An error answer was read as an empty list; it is no answer now, as a timeout already was, and the last list stands until the next real one. A stopped server still says so with an empty list of its own. (`livePlayers.ts`)

## Improved

**Signature: the live map hangs in the page's night.** BlueMap paints its sky as one flat colour times the light, so the land the flight down had hung in the page's night, under its stars and tonight's moon, landed on a dark card the moment the live map took over. The sky's shader is now given the page's night in the sky's own directions: the warm dark at the horizon up to `--sky-night-top` at the zenith; the hero's tiled pixel stars, a square of one or two of the screen's own pixels in most cells of a grid over the sky, still as the camera turns and never twinkling; and the moon as the game draws it, ten by ten with its craters, lit in the phase the real one is in tonight, low over the water left of the start view. They come out over a moment once the map is alive, after the still has handed over, as lamps come on before the stars do. By day the game's own sky comes back, deeper overhead with its lighter band on the horizon, and the stars go with the sun. The standalone viewer and the base maps' viewers (`/kort/<id>`) get the same sky. Recordings (`?upptaka`) keep BlueMap's flat sky: the flight's stills key it out, and the poster, now taken as a recording too, is what the live map hands over from. (`jod.js`, `map-poster.mjs`)

Design tests: Reason (the flight already hangs the land in this sky; the hand-over dropped it). Swap (the game's square moon in its real phase and the hero's own star tile belong to no other site). World (the game's sky, the page's tokens). System (`--sky-night-top`, `--star`, the moon tables shared with `Bits.tsx`, held together by a test). Restraint (stars at the hero's 0.55, one moon, nothing moves on its own; the day sky changes by a shade). Cost (one fragment shader on a sphere BlueMap draws anyway; no new draw call, no new texture).

**Layout**
- On a phone BlueMap's keys stood in a row at the foot on the right, across the F3 lines and the chat, and cut the last figures off the camera's position. They stand in a column at the right edge now, as the zoom keys stand on a wide screen, and the F3 lines and the chat stop short of the column while the live map is up. (`jod.css`, `badlands.css`)

**Logic**
- The moon's phases and craters moved from `Bits.tsx` into the moon's own module (`src/lib/moon.ts`), beside the phase they are drawn in; a new test holds the viewer's copy of them, its new moon and its month, and its zenith colour to the page's, and checks that both recordings run with `?upptaka`.
- The death screen's veil fades over the motion tokens' time rather than a bare `1.2s`.

**Accessibility**
- Nothing regressed: the still on a phone keeps the lantern as the one way in, and the hidden viewer takes no keys.
