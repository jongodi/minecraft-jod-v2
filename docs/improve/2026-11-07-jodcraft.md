# JOÐcraft: evolve pass, 7 November 2026

Branch `claude/friendly-faraday-5pzxzy`, on `origin/main` as of #135. A full upgrade cycle weighted toward the owner's ask: logic, layout, design, style, visuals and effects, the site in its own world, immersive and impressive. Nothing deployed.

## Brief

- **What.** The landing page of a private Paper Minecraft server for eight friends, in Icelandic. Its one job: show the server is alive and let the crew walk into their world, from a phone or a desk. Around it the notice board, a wall per member, play nights with letters, the Myndavél's photos and the owner's panel.
- **Identity.** One evening in a badlands sunset, scrolled into night. Terracotta strata, oak planks, paper slips, iron chains, lantern glass; three pixel faces; amber only on what can be pressed; no soft edge; motion in the game's whole steps; the game's own UI as the vocabulary.
- **Stack.** Next 16, React 19, plain CSS on OKLCH tokens, Redis and Vercel Blob, vitest, Vercel. The 3D map is BlueMap in JOÐ's clothes.
- **Protected.** The sunset hero, the flight and its hand-over, the rail, the rooms over the world, the hotbar, the campfire, every URL and anchor, the letters' addresses. All intact.

## How it was done

Seven audits ran in parallel, each a different lens: the client's logic, the server's, the stylesheets as a system, a live walk of the home page, a live walk of every other page, performance with Lighthouse and traces, and ideas for the evening. Every bug-finding lens was followed by a second reader told to refute each finding in the code or the running site; 46 findings survived and two were thrown out, beside nine measured performance levers and nine ideas, of which six were built. Six builders then worked in separate git worktrees on separate files, each checking its own work against a staged dev server, and the six branches merged without a conflict. A fresh review of the merged whole (the client code, the server and the service worker, the home page against the before shots, the other pages and every new Icelandic string), again with a refuting reader behind each, confirmed 18 more things to put right and threw out one; those were fixed in a last round.

## Design system

- **`--focus`.** The one global ring was lantern amber, 1.5:1 on paper and 1.1:1 on the pale woods. It is a token set per surface: the lantern on the night, `--lantern-ink` on paper, `--sign-lantern` on a sign's wood.
- **One Silkscreen cut.** The site loads only 400 but asked for 700 in four places, and the browser smeared a fake bold. 400 everywhere, `font-synthesis-weight: none` on the page, and the live map's tags at 400 to match the flight's at the hand-over.
- **The bitmap face on its grid** in the live map's skin (12 px labels to 16), the postcard's XYZ and the chip's Nýtt tag.
- **Amber only on what can be pressed**: a caption, a count, a stub and a picked night lost it. Paid calls and unearned advancements fade in ink, not opacity, and stay above AA. The duel's arena shows the lantern when the keyboard is on it.
- **Tokens.** `--bar-strata` for the strata under the bar; two bare milliseconds became `--dur-0` and `--dur-1`; a dead chip background and three split rules merged.

## Performance

- **Fonts without TrueType hinting**: 32 kB on the first screen instead of 46, the same glyphs, advances and metrics, and Chromium draws them pixel for pixel the same at every size the site sets. The slab's licence reserves the name "Alfa Slab" for the unchanged font, so the web copy is named JOD Slab inside and out; its copyright and licence stay. (`src/app/fonts/`)
- **The dust, embers and snow move on the game's 20 Hz tick**, not the screen's 60. At the idle hero on a slow phone, main-thread time fell from about 790 to 340 ms a second, and the motes move in the game's whole steps.
- **The 404 and the error screen fetch the bar, night sky and footer only when drawn**; the home's two boundary chunks went from 23.7 to 10.6 kB raw.
- **The photo upload client loads only when a photo is sent**: 19.5 kB gz less before first paint on /taflan and every wall.
- **The flight's stills, the town's lights and the map's still are immutable once versioned**: a return visit no longer sends forty revalidations. The home no longer prefetches itself.
- **The service worker** turns on navigation preload, keeps the offline page's own CSS and fonts so the lost-connection screen comes up in its clothes, and is versioned per deploy so each one drops the last's copy. A visitor on the old `jod-offline-v1` worker moves over on the first visit.

## New features

**Through the clouds.** The flight comes down through the game's cloud deck at y 192: a sparse deck of flat, hard-edged 12-block cells in the paper of the hero's clouds, the land showing through. The deck swells past the frame, thins from its edges in four whole steps over the last sixteen blocks, and is gone before the camera reaches it. The line to the town stays clear; lanterns and heads under a cloud are hidden. Four tiny pictures painted once and one transform per band per frame while the camera is over it; nothing under reduced motion. Each band is a pixelated `<img>`: an SVG or a canvas under a moving `matrix3d` went soft in Chromium. Try it: scroll from the sunset into the world.

**Reykmerki.** On the day a fire is lit for, the game's signal fire, a campfire on a hay bale, stands on the far ridge beside the J with a column of square smoke, and the footer's fire stands on a bale too. Gone once the evening is over. Placed on every phone width from 360 to 440 and every height, never across the name or the splash.

**The phantoms.** Two circle over one of the crew in the hero who has gone three of the game's days without a bed (`time_since_rest` of 72,000 ticks, the game's own threshold), coming out as the sky darkens. Pointed at or focused, the tag says "hefur ekki sofið í 4 nætur", in the right number. Only where their ring is clear of the lantern's plate.

**Við eldinn.** The crew who are in stand by the campfire at the foot of the page: up to three on a wide screen, one and a count on a phone. One who comes in walks in out of the dark in the climb's four steps.

**Hver er hvar.** A place's chip on the rail carries the heads of the crew standing at it, its postcard says "joenana og stebbias eru hérna", and the rail brings the first such chip into view once, at the first answer of who is where.

**The chat keeps listening.** On a long night it asks again every five minutes while the world is in view and when a tab hidden five minutes or more is looked at, never while hidden, and prints only what it has not printed.

Try them with staging: `STAGE=1 STAGE_NIGHT=today SECTIONS=1 node scripts/shots.mjs shots /` against a running build.
