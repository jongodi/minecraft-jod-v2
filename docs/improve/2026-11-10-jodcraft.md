# JOÐcraft: evolve pass, 10 November 2026

Branch `claude/friendly-faraday-5pzxzy`, on `origin/main` as of #136. The second pass in a week, working down the last one's list: Lighthouse 95 on the home, the duel and the walls at tablet widths, the phantoms in the world and fireworks for a full house. Nothing deployed.

## Brief

Unchanged from the 7 November report: the landing page of a private Paper server for eight friends, in Icelandic, one badlands evening scrolled into night, the game's own UI as its vocabulary. Protected: the sunset hero, the flight and its hand-over, the rail, the rooms, the hotbar, the campfire, every URL. All intact.

## How it was done

A fresh live audit, by a reader not told what this pass would change, walked every page at 1440, 1024, 768, 390, 360 and 320 with staged data and a fire tonight, played the duel to its end both ways, and tabbed through each page. It found ten defects, three of them the last pass's known ones; each was reproduced again before it was fixed. Performance was measured on production builds of #136 and of this branch served side by side, Lighthouse runs interleaved on an idle box.

## Fixed

- **On a short phone the name went under the hotbar.** At 375 by 667 (an iPhone SE), 360 by 640 and 320 px the hero's words, the lantern's plate and the night's line stood taller than the screen above the hotbar, and on a fire's day the name was off the first screen; 412 by 732 too, which the audit had not caught. On a short screen the hero starts a step under the bar, the sun's row asks for less, the name is sized by the screen's height as well as its width, the heads of who is in stand on the count's line, and the night's line leaves its heads to its words ("4 mæta" already says them). Nothing is left out; the shelf's door stays. Measured at seven phone sizes at rest and with a fire tonight. (`badlands.css`)
- **The duel's bullet hole was a black block the size of the sky.** The scene's rule for the arena's picture also laid the hole over the whole arena, through every hit to the end screen. The hole keeps its 34 px. Three fouls end in four words a size down, clear of the round's label; in a narrow side column the round slips are ledger rows instead of breaking "Umferð / III"; under 360 px the moon, wider than the gap between the labels, is left out. (`badlands.css`, `QuickDraw.tsx`)
- **The keyboard took focus onto a poster all but out of sight.** The browser scrolls a focused element in only once it is wholly hidden, so the crew room's fifth poster took the ring with 42 px showing under the rail's arrow. A rail brings the item holding keyboard focus in whole, clear of its arrows; every stop along the crew's posters now shows its poster whole. (`Rail.tsx`)
- **Small, at the edges.** A wall's skin frame stretched to the name's height on a phone and left empty wood under the figure; a wrapped button broke "Þetta er / ég" (now "Þetta / er ég", for every button); a night cloud showed above the wall's poster from 760 to about 950 px; the postcard's coordinates ran past the card at 320 px with a three-figure height (the "XYZ:" goes under 360); the lit lantern on the postcard's solid button was drawn in the button's own colour.

## Performance

- **The flight after the first paint.** The flight is drawn in the browser alone, yet its code, the cloud deck and the 17 kB of camera matrices for its forty stills came down before the name could paint. The world loads it as it loads the map sheet and the rooms; the first paint keeps only whether a flight was taken, its version and its frames (`map-flight-meta.json`, written by `map:flight`, held to the flight by a test), and the compass the locator reads has a module of its own. The home's first-load script: 205.1 to 192.8 kB compressed.
- **Mounted once the page has loaded and the browser is idle.** Loaded lazily, the flight still mounted right behind hydration, and on a throttled phone the two were one long task. Its stills wait for the load anyway; mounted then, the home's largest paint comes earlier on every run.

## New features

**Kvöldið er hafið.** When a fire's hour arrives while the page is open, or the page is first looked at within the night's first quarter of an hour, the game's /title comes up over whatever is showing: the words cut from the strata as the name is, the night's note and "3 af 3 mætt" under them, both on one hard band of the night's deepest dark so they read over a phone's lantern plate. The game's timing in its own ticks (in over ten, held seventy, out over twenty), once a night in a browser, never over a dialog, said once in a live region; still under reduced motion. Try it: plan a fire for a few minutes from now and keep the page open.

**A full house.** On a night that is going, once every one of two or more who said yes has come, the campfire's sky gets the New Year's fireworks for the rest of the evening.

**The phantoms in the world.** The phantoms over a member who has not slept follow them down: their head on the land carries the same two, out at once since it is night there, their eyes the game's green over the dark town.

Design tests for all three: Reason (the night's two moments, and a joke the crew already get, carried where it was missing). Swap (the game's own /title, fireworks and phantoms; nothing here fits another site). System (the name's strata, `--scrim-deep`, the Particles' New Year mode, the hero's phantom sprite). Restraint (each comes only when a fact is true, and goes). Cost (nothing asked of the server; one fixed layer for five seconds; the fireworks in the canvas already drawing).

## Not done

- **The stylesheet split.** The home met 95 without it, and its gain is the inner pages' first paint (/crew and /taflan stand at 93 to 94). Moving the sunset's and the world's rules out of the sheet every page loads changes their order against the rules left behind, and across 3,300 lines a tie in specificity could flip on the home with no test to catch it. It wants a pass of its own, with an order-preserving extraction and pixel diffs of every page.
- **A 320 by 568 phone on a fire's day** still has its name 50 px under the bar: the four-inch phone, rare now; hiding a line of the plate for it was not worth it.
- **The postcard's solid button** could not be seen here: the sandbox's places have no coordinates in the world, so the button that flies to one is not drawn. Its fix is two colour rules.
- **Ideas left on the shelf**: the game's toast for a member's own advancement; the game's subtitles for the wind and fire; the admin's two root layouts.
- **Not tested here**: the night's title and the fireworks on a real night, the phantoms over real players (both staged); the cloud deck in Safari and Firefox.
