# JOÐcraft: improve pass, 31 October 2026

Branch `improve/oct-31-evening`, on `origin/main` as of #133. Brief from Jón Goði: more immersive and in-theme, features that fully work and are simple to understand, high performance. Nothing pushed or deployed.

## Brief

- **What.** The landing page of a private Paper Minecraft server for eight friends, in Icelandic. Its one job: show the server is alive and let the crew walk into their world, from a phone or a desk.
- **Identity.** One evening in a badlands sunset, scrolled into night. Terracotta strata, oak planks, paper slips, iron chains, lantern glass; three pixel faces; amber only on what can be pressed; nothing soft-edged; motion in the game's whole steps. The pass fell on Hrekkjavaka, so every capture has the purple sunset and the jack-o'-lanterns on the mesas.
- **Stack.** Next 16, React 19, plain CSS on OKLCH tokens, vitest, Vercel. The 3D map is BlueMap's own app in JOÐ's clothes (`public/bluemap-jod`), never edited.
- **Protected.** The sunset hero, the flight and its hand-over, the rail of places, the rooms over the world, the hotbar, the campfire, every URL and anchor, the Myndavél look. All verified intact in the after captures.

## Fixed

- **The landing froze the page for half a second.** A viewer woken behind the flight was paused from its first moment, so its first real frame fell at the landing: shaders compiled and every tile loaded on the way down went to the GPU just as the still faded, a long task of 0.4 to 0.65 s attributed to the viewer's iframe. It draws frames nobody sees now, once when its first view is ready and again whenever the page rests a moment on the way down (`jod.warm`, told by `World`), so the landing's frame has little left to do: the two long tasks fall in the hero, where the sky is the compositor's and nothing waits on the thread. (`jod.js`, `World.tsx`)
- **The still could fade into an empty canvas.** The viewer handed over 0.6 s after its map loaded whether or not a tile had come, and the page forced the hand-over after four seconds whether or not the viewer had said hello. It now hands over once anything is drawn, with three seconds for a cold cache, and the page's fallback waits for the hello. (`jod.js`, `World.tsx`)
- **A paused map left on screen went black on resize.** BlueMap sets its renderer's size then, which clears the canvas, and a paused viewer drew nothing after: a phone turned while the map rested as a still, a window resized with a room open. One frame is drawn after a resize even while paused. (`jod.js`)
- **The stars came out mid-flight, unseen.** They were tied to the viewer's own ready, which in standby fell while it was hidden; they wait for the first unpause now, which is the landing. (`jod.js`)
- **Two chips pressed fast ended at the first.** Glides had no handle, so the older one kept writing the camera after the newer had landed. A glide on the camera or the sun overtakes the one before it. (`jod.js`)
- **The duel's hit puff drifted instead of bursting.** `@keyframes b-smoke` was declared twice in `badlands.css`; the campfire's version, later in the sheet, drove the arena's too. The arena's is `b-arena-smoke`. (`badlands.css`)
- **A copy that failed said Afritað.** The fallback's result was never read; the toast now says the copy did not go through, with the address on it to be copied by hand. (`hooks.ts`)
- **A failed viewer kept night and follow state**, so Nótt showed pressed on the retry and a head had to be pressed twice. Both reset with the failure. (`World.tsx`)
- **Status lines nobody heard.** The HUD's follow note, the map's wait and failure, and the crew room's two notes were mounted already holding their words, which screen readers do not announce; each speaks through a live region that is there before the words come. (`World.tsx`, `PlayNight.tsx`)
- **The composer could wait for good** on a storage check that hung; it gives up after twelve seconds. A best draw the board could not take said nothing; it says so. A night that is over said it had begun. The fire's time rows were keyed by position, so removing the middle one handed its field and focus to the next. A wall's stats fetch never checked the answer. The ambience's resume and close promises had no handler. (`crew-upload.ts`, `QuickDraw.tsx`, `PlayNight.tsx`, `Wall.tsx`, `ambience.ts`)
- **One dropped request took the whole flight down for the visit**, runway and all. A still that fails is asked for again twice, a moment apart. (`Flight.tsx`)
- **The dust blew twice as fast on a 120 Hz screen.** The motes, embers, snow and sparks move by the time that passed, not by the frame. (`Particles.tsx`)

## Improved

**Signature: the map's loading as the game's own bar.** A wait long enough to notice was a line of text, "sæki kortið · 7 reitir", which said nothing about how far. It is written under the lantern as the game writes a world loading: the words in the bitmap face over the experience bar's own cells, filling tile by tile as the tiles asked for come in ("sæki kortið · 4 af 9 reitum"), the viewer now telling the page how many it asked for as well as how many are drawn. The bar's colours moved from six hex literals into tokens beside the chat's game colours, so the hotbar's bar and this one are one thing.

Design tests: Reason (a wait with no measure is a wait with no end; the game's answer to that is its bar). Swap (the eighteen-cell experience bar under bitmap words belongs to no other site). World (the game's own loading screen). System (the `.b-xp` block the hotbar already draws, its colours tokens now; one live region kept mounted for the words). Restraint (shown only when the wait passes 700 ms, gone with the hand-over; nothing moves but the fill). Cost (one span and one transform; the progress message no longer re-renders the world when the count has not changed).

**Signature: the landing without a hitch** (the first three fixes above together). The hand-over from the flight to the live world is the page's one signature moment, and it neither stutters nor goes dark now.

**Logic and clarity**
- The lantern's sign on a wide screen said "hleður þrívíddarkortið" whatever the state: before any scroll, under reduced motion, beside a failure. It says what a press does until the map is asked for, that the map is on its way while it is, and that it is here once it is.
- A followed player who leaves the game is said to have ("fór úr leiknum"); the viewer reported it and the page never read it. The Nótt tip follows the state ("Sólin kemur upp aftur" while it is night). Escape folds the painted map away, one layer at a time like everything else over the world.
- A tablet with a touch screen (an iPad on its side) takes the phone's whole-screen path rather than a map in the page that takes every drag.
- Gæði remembers a Venjulegt picked by hand (it was eased down again on the next visit), and its help says Venjulegt adapts to the device.
- The splash's two English words are the game's and the site's own: "Með beltisdýri!", "Eyðimörk við sólsetur!". The splash reads the fires as they stand now, not as the page was drawn. The annals' heading names the day as the fire's does, in the nominative, with the hour under it. The locator says "rétt hjá" within ten blocks instead of "0 kubbar".
- The board's count ran one five-minute timer per reader (the bar's door on every page, the board once open); one timer serves them all. A member's slow seen-mark can no longer land after a sign-out's quick one.

**Performance**
- The flight lets its decoded stills go once landed (six 1280×720 bitmaps stood under the live map for as long as it ran), and no longer lights a rail chip for every pin that slides under a resting pointer while the camera moves (each one measured and smooth-scrolled the rail inside the scroll's frame).
- The bar's plank is painted once on a layer under its contents and faded in; it transitioned `background` from nothing to a gradient (which snaps) and `box-shadow` (which paints).
- The lit roll-call poster's glow was two blurred drop-shadows filtered on every frame of its swing; it is a pool of lantern light painted once behind the poster.

**Design system**
- Focus on paper inputs and the composer's select was a faint glow of the lantern on paper (1.5:1); it is a solid ring of the lantern's ink on `:focus-visible`. The composer's @name buttons sit on the board's own oak (4.9:1) rather than the grain's darker shade (4.0). Selected text is paper on the strata's red (5.4:1), not the orange (4.1).
- The snow's two colours and the admin table's two rules are tokens in oklch; the sky's Halloween stops and the death screen's veil are noted below as left.
- Spacing that fell between the art's pixels (0.3rem, 0.15rem, 0.35rem, 0.6rem) is on the grid; the flight's pin labels are the bitmap face at its own 16 px rather than 12; the full-screen plank's note and the chosen lantern's hint are the text face at 14 px rather than the bitmap face at 9.6; the safe-area insets read the token; two rule blocks written in both the phone's and the wide screen's media queries are written once.

**Layout and accessibility**
- The HUD's heads (press to follow) have a 32 px hit area in place of 25. The locator's base lanterns are not keys for a thumb: a mark a few pixels wide that led straight to another map was pressed by mistake, and the rail links the bases. The full-screen plank's back key is a thumb's height.

## Not done

- Lighthouse 95: the median held where it was. The LCP is the wordmark's paint behind the framework's runtime, as the two earlier reports found; nothing on this pass's list touched the critical path.
- The climb animates `clip-path` (the figure cut away below the plateau's edge): a deliberate effect on one or two small figures once per state change, left as it is.
- The call poster's stamp rotates bitmap text 12° and multiplies onto the paper: the askew stamp is the design (DESIGN.md), and one stamp per done call is not a cost; left.
- The cursor light's `mix-blend-mode: screen`: the pointer probe measured it at 6 to 8 ms a frame, and the blend is what makes it light rather than a disc; left.
- The sky's Halloween stops and the death screen's veil as tokens: hex and oklch literals in `badlands.css` beside the tokens' sRGB sky stops; moving them is paperwork with no visible gain, left for a tokens-only pass.
- The admin panel's six one-off type sizes: out of the evening's scope.
- "átta" (eight) hard-coded in five lines of prose: true today; a number-word helper for one word was not worth its weight.
- BlueMap's own loop keeps running at 60 Hz while the viewer is paused: its render loop is bound before the skin runs, so it cannot be slowed from outside without editing the viewer, which the sync would undo.
- A hint for the phone's icon-only HUD tools (folded map, picture): a new row of UI, so a feature; the words are there for screen readers.
