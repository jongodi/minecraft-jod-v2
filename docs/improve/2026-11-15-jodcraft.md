# JOÐcraft: evolve pass, 15 November 2026

Branch `claude/charming-knuth-kj3nt6`, on `origin/main` as of #138. A full cycle with no focus given. Nothing deployed.

## Brief

- **What.** The Icelandic home of a private Paper Minecraft server for eight friends. Its one job: show the server is alive and let the crew walk into their world. Around it the notice board, a wall per member, play nights with letters, the Myndavél's photos and the owner's panel.
- **Identity.** One evening in a badlands sunset, scrolled into night. Terracotta strata, oak planks, paper slips, lantern glass; three pixel faces; amber only on what can be pressed; no soft edge; motion in whole steps; the game's own UI as the vocabulary.
- **Stack.** Next 16, React 19, plain CSS on OKLCH tokens, Redis and Vercel Blob, vitest, Vercel.
- **Protected.** The sunset hero, the flight, the rail, the rooms over the world, the hotbar, the campfire, every URL and anchor. All intact.

## How it was done

The site was built and served against a local Redis and walked at 390 and 1440 px, signed in and out, with real signs on the board. Two readers audited the client and the server code in parallel, each told to refute its own findings before reporting. Every finding below was read again in the code, and where it could be staged it was reproduced before the change and checked after: a page read with the browser's clock two days on, every script failing after load, Lighthouse with a night planned.

## Fixed

**What a kept page says**

- **A page kept by the CDN said yesterday's words.** The hero's night line and the lantern's row suppress the hydration warning, and React 19 keeps the server's text in such a node. A page kept from the day before said "Þriðjudag 17. nóv." on the day itself where it should count down to tonight, and a lantern said "síðast kveikt í dag" the morning after. Both are put up anew once the page is the browser's. (`NightLine.tsx`, `StatusLantern.tsx`, `Notice.tsx`)
- **/crew threw React error #418 for the first visitor of most days.** The roll call read the clock as it drew. (`RollCall.tsx`)
- **The night line pushed the roll call and the board down.** On /crew and /taflan it was asked for by the browser and came a moment in. Both pages are drawn with the nights now. (`crew/page.tsx`, `taflan/page.tsx`)
- **A part of the page that could not be fetched took the page with it.** A phone without signal that pressed Myndir, or a tab left open across a deploy, got the error screen in place of the whole page, and the same press did it again. Each lazy part has a stand-in. (`lost.tsx`)

**What could not be pressed, heard or read**

- "Afrita hlekk á þessa sýn" on the map's F3 lines could not be tapped at all.
- "Velja myndir" stayed disabled for good after one missed answer from the store.
- The duel's call was said from inside a button, where a screen reader does not read it.
- The small print in the rooms was 4.27 to 4.46:1; it is 4.73 to 4.94.
- The calendar's arrows showed focus by a fill of 1.3:1; they carry the ring.
- A member's name on a slip stood 3 px above the rest of its line.
- A visitor read "Þú átt eftir" over someone else's advancements.

**The server**

- **Sign-in letters had no cap per member.** One address going round the crew sent each of them some fifty a day. One a quarter of an hour and five a day now, whoever asks. (`api/crew/invite`; four tests)
- **One address the post office refuses kept every night letter from everyone.** A batch refused as invalid is sent again letter by letter. (`email.ts`; three tests)
- **The five-minute job never decided a night** when mail was on and nobody took the nights' letters. (`night-mail.ts`; test)
- **A made-up path under /bluemap cost a call to exaroton** with the owner's key. Paths outside `maps/` are answered here. (`bluemap/[[...path]]`)
- **The production dependency tree audits clean** (undici, under `@vercel/blob`).

**Copy.** "Endurhladdu" for "Endurhlaðaðu"; hours as klukkustundir where "tími" also meant a time offered; a lantern that would not go out no longer says it could not be lit; "Ný mynd: Kastali Goða" leaves the place's name unbent.

## New features

**Dagatal í áskrift.** Under the fires in the crew's room: "Öll kvöldin í dagatalið þitt". One calendar, `/kvold/dagatal.ics`, holds every fire whose time is chosen and the nights of the annals. A phone that subscribes looks again on its own, so a new fire is in the calendar without anyone adding it. Try it: open `/#hopur` and press the link, or paste the copied address into a calendar on the web.

**Áfangar.** The round numbers a member passes are posted on the board: the hundredth death, the five hundredth hour ("það eru rúmir 20 sólarhringar"), ten thousand mobs, a thousand kilometres on foot. Read off the daily copies; the daily job's first run looks back sixty days, so the board starts with the last weeks' marks. Try it: after the next daily job, open `/taflan?syna=vidburdir`.

**Afrek sama kvöld.** The crew's advancements are read while they play (when one is first seen in, every half hour, and once as the last one leaves), so an advancement won at nine is on the board, in the chat and in the member's own toast that evening, not the day after. This was the last pass's third item.

**For the next pass.** `npm run seed` pins real signs on a local board through the site's own API and prints each member's cookie; `shots.mjs` takes `COOKIE=`. `scripts/stale.mjs` reads a page with the clock two days on and fails on a hydration error.

## Not done

- **The image optimizer accepts any Vercel Blob store and any local route** (`next.config.mjs`, `remotePatterns` and `localPatterns`). Anyone can have this site resize pictures from a store of their own, each a metered transformation. The fix is to name this project's store only and to list `/screenshots/**`, `/api/blob/**` and the map's still. Left because pictures stored under an older public address cannot be seen from here, and a wrong pattern breaks them in production. The owner's call, on a preview deploy.
- **A made-up tile under /bluemap/maps/ still costs exaroton two calls while the server runs.** The live map cannot be staged here; narrowing the route to the two live files is the next step, on a preview deploy.
- **A print taken down stays fetchable for a year through the optimizer** (`minimumCacheTTL`). Lowering it re-optimizes the bundled screenshots as often; a trade for the owner.
- **With Redis down, public reads answer an empty board with 200** and writes say "sign in". A strict read for the API routes would say 503; it touches every wall route and was left for a pass of its own.
- **/api/blob asks the store for any made-up name** under `crew/` or `gallery/`. A stricter pattern risked turning away names the Myndavél or the gallery use.
- **Amber on five things that cannot be pressed** ("Eltir X", a slip's data line, a closed question's winner, the "Til þín" tag, an upload's bar). Each reads as a thing lit rather than a thing to press, and may be meant; listed for the owner's eye rather than changed.
- **Not seen on an iPhone**, as before: the 11 November fixes, and this pass's webcal link.
- **Still open**: whether `CREW_TOKEN_<NAME>` should stop working once a member sets a password; the `badlands.css` reorder.

## Second round, the same day

Asked to go on, with the Icelandic itself as the focus: correct, real and casual, nothing oddly worded.

**How.** Every string the site says was pulled out of the source (about 1,400 of them, the letters and the server's refusals included) and read through. Most of it was already good; the verses, the game's own terms and the site's neuter plural ("flest", "öll", "hin") were left as they are.

**The wording (about sixty changes)**

- **The play nights.** "Ég kemst" for "Ég get", "komast" for "geta". The fire form says what to do ("Stingdu upp á einum til þremur tímum…") where it listed rules. "Tíminn sem flest komast á verður valinn sjálfkrafa" for "Kvöldið velst sjálft, sá tími sem flest geta". "Oftast er spilað á mánudagskvöldum kl. 20". The server's refusals are whole sentences ("Það logar þegar bál þetta kvöld.").
- **The stat labels.** "Lifað samfleytt" and "Vakað samfleytt" for "Tími frá síðasta dauða" and "Tími frá því síðast var sofið"; "Vegalengd á eigin fótum"; "Skaði tekinn á móti veittum".
- **The duel.** A round drawn too early "telst ekki" and is "Ógilt"; it was "fallin", as an exam is. "Nafnbót" for the rank.
- **The map.** "þrívíddarkortið er tilbúið · smelltu til að skoða", "Sleppa kortinu", "kortið hlóðst ekki".
- **The wall's slips** began mid-sentence in lower case and went on in clipped notes. "Notaðu lykilorðið sem þú valdir þér á veggnum. Áttu ekki lykilorð eða manstu það ekki?", "Ef netfangið er autt færðu engan póst."
- **Here and there.** The hero: "síðan sumarið 2024. Aðeins fyrir boðsgesti." A stack is a "stafli". The letters: "Hvenær kemstu?", "Þá geturðu hent þessu bréfi", "kallar hópinn saman". The calendar line from the first round: "Fáðu öll kvöldin í dagatalið þitt. Ný bál bætast þá sjálfkrafa við."

**Fixed**

- Pinning gives up after half a minute and says so; while it hung the slot was held and a reload was the only way on. Nafn og netfang gives up after a quarter of a minute.
- Signing in on a wall keeps the keyboard's place.
- On a phone the fire form's day ends where the time and its ✕ end under it.

**New: Næstu áfangar.** Under the charges on a wall's poster, the two round numbers nearest ahead and what is left to each: "1.000 verur felldar … 353", "100. dauðsfallið … 7". The board tells a mark the day it is passed; the poster says which are coming. Try it: open any wall, and on a phone unfold the poster.

**Numbers.** Tests 692 to 697. Lighthouse mobile, one more run of three: home 94 (LCP 3.1 s), /taflan 94, /crew 94 with a layout shift of 0.018. No sideways scroll at 390 or 1440 px; the kept-page check passes.

**Looked at and left.** The compass needle on the locator bar is out of the keyboard's reach on purpose (the bar is hidden from it and the HUD is the way); "plakat" and "spjald" are two things on a wall, not one word used two ways. Everything under *Not done* above still stands.
