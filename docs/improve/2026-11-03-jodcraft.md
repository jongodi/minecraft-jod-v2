# JOÐcraft: evolve pass, 3 November 2026

Branch `feat/nov-03-evolve`, on `origin/main` as of #134. A full upgrade cycle: logic, performance, the design system and one new feature. Nothing pushed or deployed.

## Brief

- **What.** The landing page of a private Paper Minecraft server for eight friends, in Icelandic. Its one job: show the server is alive and let the crew walk into their world, from a phone or a desk. Around it: a notice board, a wall per member, play nights with letters, the Myndavél's photos, and the owner's panel.
- **Identity.** One evening in a badlands sunset, scrolled into night. Terracotta strata, oak planks, paper slips, iron chains, lantern glass; three pixel faces; amber only on what can be pressed; no soft edge anywhere; motion in the game's whole steps. The game's own UI is the vocabulary: the hotbar, the experience bar, the chat, the title screen's splash.
- **Stack.** Next 16, React 19, plain CSS on OKLCH tokens, Redis and Vercel Blob, Resend for the letters, vitest, Vercel. The 3D map is BlueMap's own app in JOÐ's clothes (`public/bluemap-jod`).
- **Protected.** The sunset hero, the flight and its hand-over, the rail of places, the rooms over the world, the hotbar, the campfire, every URL and anchor, the letters' addresses, the Myndavél look. All intact; the sign-in links' address is unchanged.

## Audit

Three audits were run in parallel (the server side, the client components, the stylesheets) against the three October passes, so nothing already fixed was reported again. Each finding below was read in the code before it went on the plan.

## Fixed

**The server side**

- **The admin's cookie was the token.** The panel's session cookie carried `ADMIN_TOKEN` as it was, so one copied from a device or a log opened the panel for good, whatever the cookie's eight hours said. It is a signed note of when it ends now (`<ends>.<HMAC under the token>`), checked in the proxy and every admin route through the Web Crypto API, which both runtimes have. Signing in once more is the only change the admin sees. (`auth.ts`, `api/admin/auth`, `proxy.ts`; five tests)
- **A member's sign-in forgot its address's count but not the account's.** Twenty failed tries from anywhere kept the member out for an hour after they had signed in. Cleared on success, as the address's is. (`api/crew/auth`; one test)
- **A gallery that would not parse was seeded over.** The store's gallery was read with the lenient read, which answers null for a value that does not parse as it does for no key, and the bundled seed was written over the admin's gallery. The strict read raises it. (`gallery.ts`)
- **A vote from a letter had no write limit.** The count ran only for a session, and a vote by key has none, so a forwarded letter could write without limit until the night began. Counted for whoever is writing. (`api/playnight`)
- **A night letter's claim could be kept for good.** The claim was marked before the letter was made and released only on a sent answer of false; a throw between left the letter marked sent, and it never went. The claim is given back on a throw too. (`night-mail.ts`)
- **A sign with too many pictures was quietly cut** to twelve after they were already uploaded, so it looked like a lost upload. Turned away with the limit named. (`api/crew/[username]/entries`)
- **Small corrections.** The hours in the world are counted from the ticks (eight floored hours lost most of a day); an unknown map-bases action is a 400, not an upload that starts the workflow; exaroton's refusals on a datapack refresh are told apart from a folder not found; a picture registers from this project's own store only; the site's address is read the way the letters read it (an empty value or a trailing slash no longer makes a bad link); a dead type and a stale comment go.

**The page**

- **An arrival in the world lands at once.** `showWorld(false)` asked for behavior `auto`, which takes the stylesheet's smooth scroll: a room's link or a shared place glided down from the sunset and was cut short by the runway being laid. `instant` is what it meant. (`hooks.ts`)
- **Escape closes one layer at a time.** With the live map held and the painted map drawn over it, one Escape folded the map and let go of the live map at once; in the overlay full screen it folded the map and closed the whole screen. (`World.tsx`)
- **The land, the locator and the flight hold still through a status answer.** The hero's land was not memoised and got a fresh list of who is in on every poll; the locator got a fresh home mark and a fresh empty list each render, so its memo and the flight's never held, and each re-ran its effect (a layout read and a query of its marks) on every status tick. Out of view, the live list is let go, so heads no longer stand where people were. (`Mesa.tsx`, `Hero.tsx`, `livePlayers.ts`, `World.tsx`)
- **A door's history entry keeps what the other layers wrote in it.** The full screen and the layers mark the history state as their own, and a door wrote the state afresh: on a phone, a room opened in the full screen, a door pressed and then Loka left a dead Back. (`BadlandsHome.tsx`)
- **The roll call's and the room's questions are capped**, and a lost stats answer ends the wait: every poster kept its "sæki" for good. (`RollCall.tsx`, `Crew.tsx`)
- **The board says when it could not fetch the newest** over a board already drawn, with the way to try again; it was silent. (`Board.tsx`)
- **The page's own play-night answer is asked for once.** A signed-out visitor's answer names nobody, so every line that read it (the hero, the night's line, the fires, the annals) asked again on mount. (`night.ts`)
- **Deila without a clipboard offers the link** (it did nothing on http or an old webview), **eyða pressed twice sends once**, the wall's dialogs bind their Escape once, a given item's pop ends under reduced motion (its animation was `none`, so the end never fired), the room's list of walls is not written after the room is gone. (`Print.tsx`, `Wall.tsx`, `wall.css`, `Crew.tsx`)
- **Two live regions a screen reader can hear.** The admin's status line was mounted already holding its words, which is not announced; it is there empty and filled. The duel's call lived inside the arena, a button, whose children are presentational to a screen reader; it is said beside the arena. The board's string marking where the visitor last looked was a separator among list items, which breaks a screen reader's count; it is an item. (`ui.tsx`, `QuickDraw.tsx`, `Board.tsx`)
- What nothing imports is not exported; two comments pointed the wrong way.

## Improved

**Signature: a sign-in link opened spends nothing; the member's own press does.** Mail scanners and chat previews open links to look at them, and each look spent one of the link's five uses and signed a scanner in (the night letters' answer links learnt this already; the sign-in links had not). The link now opens the roll call with a slip naming the member, in the welcome's own paper, and their press on it spends the use, signs the browser in for a year and takes them to their wall. A spent link says so on the press as on the roll call. The letters' address is unchanged. (`api/crew/invite`, `crew-access.ts`, `RollCall.tsx`; four tests)

Design tests: Reason (a look is not a press; the one who presses is the one who is signed in). Swap (the slip is the welcome's paper on the night, in the site's own words). World (a paper slip on the board). System (the `.w-welcome` paper the wall already uses, one button). Restraint (one line and one press). Cost (one redirect fewer at the first visit, one POST on the press).

**Performance**
- The roll call's largest paint was its most wanted poster's print, and it was lazy like the rest: the browser waited for layout before asking, and the page's largest paint came two seconds late. Eager and high priority for that one print. Lighthouse on `/crew` went from 86 to the high nineties (below).
- The status poll no longer redraws the hero's land, the locator or the flight (above).

**Design system**
- No soft edges: five blurred drop-shadows went. The pin's lamp and the skin's by a pixel became a hard pixel straight down, as every lift on the site is; the locator's base lanterns and the wall's corner pin keep their own lit light; the roll call's poster lamp lost a twelve-pixel bloom filtered on every swing, beside the pool already painted for it.
- The bar's lanterns land on whole pixels: the doors', the status's and the slots' were drawn in a 17.6 by 26.4 pixel box for a 12 by 18 sprite, so no pixel of the drawing landed on a device pixel; 18 by 27 now.
- Focus rings on the three fields written straight on a board (the sign's text, a draft's caption, a reply), which set `outline: none` and showed focus only by recolouring the rule under them.
- The flame's easing is one named motion (`--ease-flicker`), the only easing on the site that was not a token.
- The admin's sheet, which never had the October token pass: the soft text's alpha from the token (fourteen literals), the bitmap face at its 16 px grid (eleven rules stood at nine to fourteen pixels), the panel's own ramp step on the grid, a solid focus ring on its inputs where a glow sat at 1.5:1, and four breakpoints spelled as the site's.
- The compass needle in the site's own inks; the Yule lad's two colours named as his; two breakpoints (767, 359) spelled as their families.

## New feature

**Síðast inni: each member's last evening in the game.** The status check already noted when the server last burned and who was in; it now notes each of the crew as seen at that moment too, in one hash (`src/lib/crew-seen.ts`, written at the same beat, every few minutes or when who is in changes). The roll call writes "síðast inni í gær kl. 21:40" under a member's charges, and the crew's room "inni fyrir 3 dögum" under a head that is not in, where the photo count stood. The lantern still says who is in now. Nothing is shown without the store (so not in development), and nothing for a member never seen since this pass went live.

Try it: `/crew`, and the crew's room (`/#hopur`), once the server has burned with someone in.

Design tests: Reason (the one question the room is asked about someone who is not there). Swap (a line of small print on a wanted poster). World (the poster's own meta line, the faint ink). System (`--fs-sm` in `--ink-faint`, `sinceAt` and `formatAge` as the rest of the site tells time). Restraint (one line, only under a head that is not in). Cost (one HSET beside a write that already happens; one HGETALL on the crew list).

## Not done

- The status store hands every subscriber a new object on every poll, since `checkedAt` moves; the lantern reads it to say how old its answer is, so the reference cannot simply be kept. Memoising the land and the lists below it took the cost out of the two re-renders that mattered; a separate clock store is the clean fix, left.
- jod.css carries the palette as hex (the viewer's iframe cannot read the page's tokens) and a few unnamed near-whites and alphas of its own, as do the four Satori routes. One `palette.ts` of hex derived from the OKLCH, with a test keeping the copies honest, is the right shape and a pass of its own.
- The swing durations (1.2, 1.3, 1.5, 1.8 s for four hung things settling) and the world's loops (64 s amble, the smoke) are tuned by eye and left as literals; folding them into one token would change the feel for the sake of a table.
- The map editor re-renders on every pointer move over the map, and its number fields write a history step per keystroke; the owner's tool, left for a pass of its own.
- The admin panel replaces its facts with the error on one failed poll; left.
- A tap on a locator mark on a phone saying the distance in the chat (noted by three passes): the marks are fourteen pixels wide on a bar that is pointer-only by design, so a thumb would need a bar of its own; not built.
- The site's hotbar labels under 360 px stand the bitmap face at 12 px, off its grid, because 16 px overflows a slot there; left as a documented trade.
